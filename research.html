Dream bigger, feel worse? The side effects of boosting aspirations

Working paper · 2026
(with Selene Ghisolfi)
→

Abstract

We study the impact of a brief, low-cost intervention on aspirations, soft skills and well-being. 297 primary and middle school students from fragile backgrounds in Naples, Italy, were randomized into viewing a short optimistic video message from a role model. We find that exposure to the video significantly increases short-term self-reported educational aspirations, especially among immigrant students. However, it causes a shift in self-efficacy and grit, a decline in mood, and no measurable improvement in long-term academic performance. This study shows how a scalable role model intervention impacting short-run aspirations creates backlashes on students' well-being and attitudes.
